What to keep in mind

The main points to check before committing.

The empty-floor price is our estimate

Route A depends on a buyer paying AED 48-52M at the end of 2027. At about AED 44M the investor only gets the money back.

Leasing the offices takes time

Route B assumes each half opens 70% full and reaches 95% in four months. At 85% occupancy and a lower desk price, the year-5 return falls to about 19.1%.

The later sale price

Route B sells the floor as an income-producing asset at about AED 6,130 per sq ft. Buyers will price that income, so the final value moves with the market and the cap rate.

Floor area to confirm

The plan uses 23,000 sq ft; the two leases cover 20,785 sq ft. The title deed confirms the figure before signing, and the desk count and fit-out budget follow it.

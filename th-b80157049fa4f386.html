  • The market is full. 93.4% city-wide and about 99% at prime and Grade A; no offices were completed in Q2 2026.
  • Rents are moving. City-wide asking rents rose 15% in the quarter and 33% in a year to AED 2,182/sqm; prime ADGM now exceeds AED 6,000/sqm.
  • Demand is policy-driven. ADGM licences +30% and workforce +51% in 2025; funds, asset managers and banks lead.
  • Relief is years away. Under 100,000 sqm a year city-wide until 2029; inside ADGM, One Maryah Place is the only large delivery and it will arrive largely pre-let.
  • Al Reem is the relief valve. Same jurisdiction, regulator and tenant pool, exempt from the June 2026 rent freeze, at AED 1,400/sqm against 6,000 on Al Maryah.

The strategy

Buy Grade A floors on Al Reem, fit them out in six months as turnkey suites of 200-500 sqm, lease them to ADGM-licensed firms on multi-year terms, and sell the stabilised income to yield buyers before the 2029 supply wave.

Why it pays

A serviced suite earns about AED 5,900/sqm on a floor that lets at AED 1,600/sqm as shell. In the illustrative case, year-2 NOI is a 16.1% yield on total cost, sold at a 10% cap: a spread of about 600 bps.

Illustrative returns

A full floor in Sky Tower at AED 48M: 30.1% investor IRR and 1.63x over two years, all-equity; the 8% preferred return holds to 66% occupancy or a 16.4% exit cap rate.

What has to go rightLease-up speed and exit pricing. Both are measured on the risk slides; neither depends on further rent growth.

04 · Al Reem
Palm & Peak · October 2026 · For the intended recipient only

Al Reem became part of ADGM in 2023, its property register moved to ADGM in 2025, and in June 2026 it was exempt from the emirate-wide rent freeze

Regulatory timeline
2013
ADGM established on Al Maryah Island

Common-law financial free zone with its own courts, regulator (FSRA) and Registration Authority.

24 April 2023
Cabinet Resolution 41 of 2023

Jurisdiction extended to Al Reem; ADGM's area grows roughly tenfold.

End 2024 - Feb 2025
Licence migration complete

Over 1,100 Al Reem entities move to ADGM licences.

1 January 2025
Property register transfers to ADGM

ADGM Real Property Regulations 2024 govern title and leasing on Al Reem; no statutory rent cap.

2-3 June 2026
ADREC rent freeze: 0% increase cap

All renewals across Abu Dhabi; ADGM, including Al Reem, exempt.

0%
Permitted rent increase, Abu Dhabi mainland, from June 2026
Exempt
ADGM jurisdiction: Al Maryah and Al Reem

Why it matters. An owner on Al Reem keeps contractual escalations and open-market renewals; an owner anywhere else in the emirate does not. In Cushman & Wakefield's words, the exemption enables rents "to respond to strong market fundamentals".

  • Tenants: every ADGM-licensed firm needs a registered address in the jurisdiction; Al Reem is the only part of ADGM with space.
  • Enforcement: leases register with ADGM and are enforceable in ADGM courts.
  • Tax: an ADGM SPV can be a qualifying free-zone person; income from ADGM-licensed tenants may qualify for 0% corporate tax, subject to substance rules and advice.
Source: UAE Cabinet Resolution 41/2023; ADGM Registration Authority (Mar 2024, Feb 2025); ADGM Real Property Regulations 2024; ADREC (3 Jun 2026); Cushman & Wakefield Abu Dhabi Office MarketBeat Q2 2026.

04 · Al Reem
Palm & Peak · October 2026 · For the intended recipient only

A serviced suite on Al Reem earns about AED 5,900/sqm, 3.7x the island's Grade A shell rent and close to what ADGM asks before any fit-out

Rent and serviced revenue, AED per sqm per year
Serviced suite = AED 3,200 per workstation a month at 6.5 sqm per workstation, all-inclusive (Sky Tower model v7). Rents are asking, including service charge.
3.7xServiced revenue over Al Reem Grade A shell rent
  • Why tenants pay it. No fit-out capex, no 9-12 month build, a registered ADGM address from day one, and a lower all-in cost than shell space on Al Maryah once fit-out is counted.
  • Why the spread persists. UAE flex is 4-6% of office stock against about 12% in London; serviced supply on Al Reem is thin and the operators already trading there are full.
  • Building selection matters. Target Grade A towers with parking, mall access and serviced demand already proven, such as Sky Tower.

05 · Strategy
Palm & Peak · October 2026 · For the intended recipient only

The return is a development spread: year-2 NOI is a 16.1% yield on total cost, and a buyer at a 10% cap pays AED 108.1M for it

Value bridge, illustrative Sky Tower floor, AED M
Exit value = year-2 NOI before fee of AED 10.8M at a 10% cap, before 2% sale costs. AED 3.1M of the cost is VAT refunded in year 1.
1
Buy

A full Grade A floor on Al Reem, inside ADGM, in a tower with parking and proven serviced demand.

2
Fit out in six months

4-6 turnkey suites of 200-500 sqm with shared reception, boardrooms and pantry, priced all-inclusive.

3
Lease from day one

Firms register their ADGM address before moving in; two-to-five-year agreements with fixed 5% escalation.

4
Operate

Lean staffing, all-inclusive pricing, renewals priced to market, which ADGM law permits.

5
Sell or hold

Sell the contracted income to a yield buyer before the 2029 supply wave, or hold for an 11-12% cash yield.

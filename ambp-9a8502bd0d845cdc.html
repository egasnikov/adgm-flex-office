The Product - Corporate Suites on a Full Floor

The floor is fitted out as fully furnished private suites of 100-500 sqm around a shared reception, boardroom, meeting rooms and lounge, and let to corporate occupiers on 2-5 year all-inclusive agreements. The operator carries the fit-out, the licence, staffing and operations; the landlord receives a single head lease on the whole floor from one covenant.

Conventional Grade A fitted space on Al Maryah: full-floor comps AED 4,350-4,400/sqm (Aug 2026 broker channel), prime headline above AED 6,000/sqm (Cushman & Wakefield H1 2026). The DIY stack uses the mid-point and the same fit-out basis as this plan, amortised over 5 years.

Method and assumptions. Monthly engine, 2,500 sqm lettable (26,910 sq ft), common areas excluded, on Al Maryah Island; building name withheld pending the landlord's approval. Head lease at AED 7.5M in year 1 (AED 3,000/sqm), escalation as set (5% base), security deposit of 10% of annual rent returned at expiry, service charge of 5% of rent (the 5% VAT on it is recoverable and excluded), chiller per meter and water and electricity for the tenant's account (inside the utilities line). Rent-free period as offered by the landlord on a 7-10 year term: head rent waived for the chosen number of months from signing, service charge still payable. Agency fee per the signed Terms of Business, modelled at 10% of year-1 rent at signing. Fit-out AED 550/sq ft plus 5% contingency; 5% VAT on fit-out funded at signing and recovered in lease year 1. Set-up, legal and launch marketing AED 690,000. Two months of pre-opening payroll. Trading opens at 60% of the floor after the fit-out period, rising 5 points a month to stabilisation; 1% credit loss. Revenue is the all-inclusive rate per sqm of lettable area times occupancy, escalating on each trading anniversary. Core operating costs AED 133,000 a month at 2026 prices, built bottom-up for this floor (see table), 3% escalation; ADGM licence AED 50,000 a year; leasing commission 10% of contracted annual rent on origination and each 3-year renewal. Rent cover is operating profit before rent and service charge divided by rent plus service charge. Project figures are pre-tax and unlevered with no residual value at lease expiry; there is no management fee or profit split in this plan.
